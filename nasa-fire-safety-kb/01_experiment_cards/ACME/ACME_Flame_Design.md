## 4. Ключевые физические явления и результаты
Demonstrated soot-free quasi-steady spherical diffusion flames by tailoring stoichiometric mixture fraction (Zst) via fuel-side vs oxidizer-side inert dilution: diluting fuel / enriching oxidizer (high Zst) shifts flame sheet toward fuel side, cooling/thinning the fuel-rich pyrolysis zone to eliminate soot; diluting oxidizer (low Zst) increases soot and radiation loss leading to radiative extinction

## 5. Значение для пожарной безопасности космических аппаратов
Enables rational design of soot-free, clean-burning spacecraft and terrestrial combustors and validates radiation/soot-inception models

## 6. Научная преемственность и применение
Builds on FLEX radiative extinction physics and pairs with s-Flame to decouple soot formation, radiation loss, and Zst effects without buoyancy
