## 2. Экспериментальная установка (Hardware & Diagnostics)
ISS CIR using ACME Chamber Insert Assembly (CIA) replacing FLEX MDCA while reusing CIR chamber, optics, gas supply, and avionics; 25-50 mm flat porous stainless-steel burner

## 4. Ключевые физические явления и результаты
Flat porous burner emulates solid/liquid material heat of gasification and burning rates via controlled gaseous fuel flow; sustained burning for minutes with zero airflow in elevated-O2 atmospheres; 1g burn rate 3-4x higher than 0g

## 5. Значение для пожарной безопасности космических аппаратов
Shutting down cabin ventilation alone may not immediately extinguish fire in elevated-O2 Exploration Atmospheres; enables rapid non-destructive material flammability screening

## 6. Научная преемственность и применение
Connects gaseous burner physics to solid-fuel flammability from BASS and SoFIE; enables rapid screening of materials in 0g without consuming solid coupons
