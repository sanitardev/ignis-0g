# ACME Program Overview (6 Experiments) (ACME)

## 1. Общая информация и научная команда
* **Программа / Направление:** ACME — `Prevention / Suppression / Fundamental`
* **Модуль / Установка на МКС:** CIR (CIA)
* **Главные исследователи (PI / Co-I):** Dennis P. Stocker (NASA GRC Project Scientist); PIs: James G. Quintiere (BRE), Richard L. Axelbaum (Flame Design), Chung K. Law (s-Flame), Derek Dunn-Rankin (E-FIELD Flames), Marshall B. Long (CLD Flame), Forman A. Williams / NASA GRC (CFI-G)

## 2. Экспериментальная установка (Hardware & Diagnostics)
ISS Destiny Lab CIR rack using modular ACME Chamber Insert Assembly (CIA) that replaced FLEX MDCA while reusing CIR chamber, optical diagnostics, gas mixing, and avionics

## 4. Ключевые физические явления и результаты
Six-experiment suite (BRE, Flame Design, s-Flame, E-FIELD Flames, CLD Flame, CFI-G) studying: (1) spacecraft fire prevention & solid-material emulation (BRE), (2) soot-free flames & Zst control (Flame Design), (3) radiative extinction & instabilities (s-Flame), (4) active electric-field soot/flame control (E-FIELD), (5) elevated-pressure soot CFD benchmarks (CLD), and (6) gaseous cool flames (CFI-G)

## 5. Значение для пожарной безопасности космических аппаратов
Unified gaseous microgravity testbed bridging fundamental combustion physics, soot reduction, CFD validation, and spacecraft fire safety

## 6. Научная преемственность и применение
Direct hardware and scientific successor to FLEX inside CIR (radiative extinction & cool flames -> gas phase) and gas-phase counterpart to BASS solid-fuel flammability studies
