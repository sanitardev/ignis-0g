# BASS / BASS-II / BASS-M (BASS)

## 1. Общая информация и научная команда
* **Программа / Направление:** BASS — `Prevention`
* **Модуль / Установка на МКС:** MSG / SPICE
* **Главные исследователи (PI / Co-I):** Sandra L. Olson (PI, NASA GRC); Fletcher J. Miller (PI, San Diego State Univ.)

## 2. Экспериментальная установка (Hardware & Diagnostics)
MSG (Microgravity Science Glovebox) housing modified SPICE (Smoke Point In Coflow Experiment) small wind-tunnel duct with solid sample holders

## 4. Ключевые физические явления и результаты
U-shaped flammability boundary in 0g: absence of 1g buoyant convection (~20-30 cm/s) reduces convective cooling/blow-off at low forced flows (1-10 cm/s), creating thicker boundary layers until radiative quenching below 1-2 cm/s; SIBAL burned at 13.6% O2 (-2.4% safety margin vs 1g)

## 5. Значение для пожарной безопасности космических аппаратов
Earth upward flame-spread certification NASA-STD-6001 Test 1 is not always conservative in low-flow spacecraft HVAC regimes; restarting airflow causes rapid flare-up

## 6. Научная преемственность и применение
Demonstrates that radiative vs convective heat-loss balance in 0g governs solid flammability; complements ACME BRE (gaseous emulation of solid burning rates) and SoFIE (subatmospheric Exploration Atmospheres)
